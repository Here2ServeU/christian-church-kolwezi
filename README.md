## ⚡ Platform Modules

The platform (`platform.html`) includes 11 fully functional modules:

| # | Module | Description |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | **Dashboard** | Live metrics — members, giving, attendance, health |
| 2 | **Members** | Directory with 601 profiles, stages, tags, giving history |
| 3 | **Sermons** | Library with views, podcast links, YouTube tracking |
| 4 | **Giving** | Orange Money, Airtel, PayPal — full financial dashboard |
| 5 | **Groups** | 6 cells with health scores, leaders, attendance |
| 6 | **Events** | Calendar with RSVP tracking and capacity management |
| 7 | **Prayer Wall** | Community intercession with live prayer counter |
| 8 | **Campaigns** | Email, SMS, WhatsApp — open & click rate tracking |
| 9 | **Volunteers** | 87 volunteers across 8 teams with ratings |
| 10 | **Growth Roadmap** | All 4 phases — Phases 1 & 2 open, 3 & 4 expandable |
| 11 | **Pricing** | 3 plans with monthly/annual billing toggle |

## Key Features

- **Bilingual** — French (default) and English, switchable on every page
- **Mobile responsive** — works on any phone, tablet, or computer
- **No installation** — pure HTML/CSS/JS, opens directly in any browser
- **GitHub Pages ready** — upload and go live in under 10 minutes
- **DRC-optimized** — Orange Money, Airtel Money, WhatsApp Business built in
- **Interactive charts** — powered by Chart.js (loaded from CDN)
- **Checklist progress** — all phase steps are clickable and trackable
- **Dark theme** — professional, easy on the eyes in all lighting
